# MytheAi Sponsored Outreach Playbook

Pre-drafted materials for closing the first paid sponsorships ($200-800/mo recurring per vendor). Target: 3 vendors signed in 30 days = $600-1200 MRR baseline.

Created: 2026-10-08. For execution starting ~2026-10-11 (post 72h Plausible observation).

---

## Why this playbook

MytheAi revenue today = 100% affiliate commissions (delayed 30-90 days, 1 cancelation = revenue loss). Sponsored tier diversifies to:
- Predictable monthly recurring revenue (vs variable affiliate)
- Independent of vendor affiliate program changes (Jasper slashed commissions 50% in 2024)
- Scales linearly with vendor count, not visitor count
- /advertise page already live with pricing + legal integrity disclaimer

Realistic target 3 months: **10 vendors × $200-400 avg = $2-4k MRR** by Jan 2027.

## Weekly outreach cadence

**Monday 1h**: Send 3 cold emails from `vendor-shortlist` using templates. Log in CRM.
**Wednesday 30min**: Follow up vendors who opened but didn't reply (3-day gap).
**Friday 30min**: Review CRM, update statuses, archive dead leads, add 3 new vendors to shortlist from catalog.

**Expected funnel**:
- 10 emails sent / week
- 3-5 open (30-50% open rate)
- 1-2 reply (10-20% reply rate)
- 1 close every 2-3 weeks

**30-day realistic**: 1-2 vendors closed = $400-800 first MRR.

## Rules of engagement

1. **No fake stats**. Pitch honest traffic numbers from Plausible once observation complete. If traffic low (< 5k/mo), lead with content moat (593 tools, 214 hand-tested, 506 use-case guides) not visitor count.
2. **Lead with their review**. Every email references the specific hand-tested review we published about them. Not generic blast - personalized by proof.
3. **Editorial integrity disclosed upfront**. Mention that sponsored placement cannot buy editorial ranking. Separates us from pay-to-rank directories = premium positioning.
4. **Give them an easy "no"**. Include unsubscribe + "if not now, when?" fallback. Doesn't poison the well.
5. **Price anchor high, settle fair**. Pitch Featured Boost first ($99/week), negotiate down to Verified Badge ($29/mo) if resistant. Easier to anchor high + flex down than start low.
6. **Max 3 follow-ups**. Day 3 bump, Day 7 "closing loop", Day 14 "circling back one last time". After that, archive. Don't harass.

## Legal / ethical reminders

- Every sponsored placement MUST render `Sponsored` badge visibly (CLAUDE.md affiliate-monetization rule).
- Sponsored placements NEVER appear in Top 10 lists, Editor's Picks, or comparison winners.
- Keep invoices + contracts (even 1-line email agreement) for every closed deal.
- Delaware C-Corp recommended before closing first deal (Stripe Atlas 2 weeks setup) for liability + tax.
