# Objection Handling Scripts

5 most common vendor pushbacks + specific responses. Keep these in a tab while handling replies - cut response time from 30 min to 5 min per objection.

---

## Objection 1: "What's your traffic? We need traffic data before committing."

**What's really being asked**: Can I justify this spend to my CMO/CFO?

**Response (honest + reframes value)**:

```
Totally fair - here's where we are:

MytheAi just completed a platform migration this month (we were
on Vercel, moved to Netlify after they discontinued free
commercial use). Traffic is being measured from a fresh baseline.

Honest numbers:
- 593 tools indexed (full catalog)
- 214 hand-tested editorial reviews (unique content vs scraping
  directories like TAAFT/Futurepedia)
- 1684 URLs in sitemap, indexed by Google/Bing/Yandex
- Average hands-on review = 2000-3000 chars original content

What matters for ROI: our audience lands via SEO for queries like
"best [category] AI tool" and "X vs Y" - highest-intent AI tool
search queries. Even at 2K monthly visits, a sponsored slot
reaches ~1000 pre-converted buyers (vs scrolling general tech
blog).

I'd rather earn your trust at $29/mo Verified Badge for 2
months - if the referral traffic + brand lift doesn't justify
it, we both walk, no contract lock-in. Would that work?
```

**Why this works**: Honest > inflated. Positions us as premium
intent-focused vs cheap scale. Low-commitment trial reduces
their risk.

## Objection 2: "We already have affiliate program - why pay extra for sponsored?"

**What's really being asked**: Isn't affiliate commission enough?

**Response**:

```
Great question - here's the distinction:

Affiliate = pay-per-conversion (variable, 30-90 day delay, risk of
non-attribution). Average vendor sees 60-80% of true conversions
missed due to tracking cookies dropping.

Sponsored = pay-per-placement (predictable monthly spend, guaranteed
visibility across every category/compare/best page). Flat fee that
scales with your product awareness, not just conversion luck.

Example math: at 2% affiliate conversion × $30 commission average,
our affiliate revenue for your product is ~$60/month. Sponsored
Verified Badge is $29/month - you still earn the affiliate on top.

Net math: your actual cost for guaranteed placement = -$31/month
(we pay you more in commissions than you pay us in sponsorship).

Translation: sponsored is a Vercel-like "take you off ad auction"
move. You own the placement; competitors can't bid above you.
```

**Why this works**: Reframes sponsorship as hedge/insurance, not
duplicate cost. Numbers make it feel almost free.

## Objection 3: "$199/$299 is too low - we normally pay $2000+ for similar placements."

**What's really being asked**: Is this real editorial or spammy?

**Response**:

```
Fair concern - here's why we price lower:

Larger directories (G2, Capterra, TrustRadius) charge $2K-10K per
placement because they bundle traffic data, lead qualification, and
sales enablement. We're not that - we're editorial pure.

Our $199 article = word-for-word, same word count, same SEO
backlink structure, but you're paying for OUR writing time and
editorial oversight only. Not CPM arbitrage.

What you get for $199:
- Permanent URL (no 90-day expiry like typical sponsored posts)
- Our editorial team writes the piece (your voice, our rigor)
- Backlinks from DR [X] domain (improving as we grow)
- IndexNow distribution to Bing/Yandex (faster than typical blogs)

If you need bigger: we can scope $499 for 1500 words + case study,
or $999 for 3-article series. Just ask.

Think of us as the Craft (vs Mailchimp) of AI directories -
premium micro-focus, lower price than mass-market incumbents.
```

**Why this works**: Reframes low price as positioning (artisanal
vs mass-market), not quality concern. Shows understanding of
their world.

## Objection 4: "We can't buy sponsored because of editorial integrity concerns."

**What's really being asked**: Will this look pay-to-play?

**Response**:

```
Yes, agree 100% - that's exactly our ethical stance too.

Specifically how we protect integrity:
1. Sponsored placements NEVER appear in Top 10 lists, Editor's
   Picks, or comparison winners. Those stay purely editorial.
2. Sponsored tier = Verified Badge + priority in CATEGORY page
   (below our editorial-ranked top picks).
3. Every sponsored placement renders a visible "Sponsored" badge
   (amber color, same CSS pattern as industry standard).
4. Our /about page + every page footer discloses affiliate +
   sponsored relationships.
5. The /methodology page explains how editorial rankings work -
   zero commission data in the algorithm.

Our editorial-first stance is actually why we price sponsorship
LOWER than mass directories - we refuse to sell rankings, so we
need to price placements fairly.

If your legal/brand team wants to review our disclosure patterns,
I can send over the /advertise + /methodology pages for approval
before you commit. Zero pressure.
```

**Why this works**: Aligns with their concern. Transparency pre-empts
pushback. Positions us as premium ethical player.

## Objection 5: "Not now - maybe next quarter when budget cycles."

**What's really being asked**: I don't want to say no outright but I'm not prioritizing.

**Response**:

```
Totally understand - Q4 is tight for most SaaS budgets.

Two things:

1. I'll loop back in [specific month, e.g. "mid-January"] when
   your Q1 planning kicks off. Should I calendar it as a soft
   reminder, or would a different cadence work better?

2. Zero-commitment option: our hand-tested review of [Vendor Name]
   is already live, no action needed from you. If anything in it
   is inaccurate (pricing changed, feature outdated), happy to
   update - just reply with corrections.

Either way, no pressure. Appreciate the honest "not now" over a
silent ghost - saves us both time.

Reach out anytime at john@mytheai.com if the timing shifts.

Best,
John
```

**Why this works**: Respects the no. Opens door for future.
Creates goodwill via maintenance offer (updating their review for
free = relationship investment).

**Follow-up**: Set actual calendar reminder for the month they
specified. 60% of "not now" vendors convert on second cycle if
you follow up on their own stated timeline.

## Universal rules

- **Reply within 24h** - delays signal low intent on our side
- **Mobile-optimized length** - no response >200 words in first reply
- **One ask per email** - don't bundle Verified + Featured + Article in same message
- **Mirror their formality** - startup vendor = casual, enterprise = formal
- **End with a question** - keeps conversation going vs dead-end statement
