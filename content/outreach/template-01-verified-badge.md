# Email Template 01 - Verified Badge $29/mo

**Use for**: Mid-tier SaaS where $29/mo is comfortable, first-touch cold outreach, lowest barrier to close.

**Target reply rate**: 15-20%.
**Target close rate**: 20% of replies = ~3% of sent.

---

## Subject line variations (test 1 per batch)

- `[Vendor Name] - we hand-tested you, want to make it official?`
- `[Vendor Name] in our hand-tested top picks - partnership option?`
- `Our John Pham reviewed [Vendor Name] - quick partnership question`

Pick based on vendor formality. Scrappy startups (HeyGen, Fireflies): first variant. Established (Jasper, Metabase): second or third.

---

## Email body

```
Hi [First Name],

John Pham from MytheAi here. We're an editorial AI tools
directory - 593 tools reviewed, 214 hand-tested by our team
including a full hands-on write-up on [Vendor Name]:
https://mytheai.com/tools/[slug]

The review is positive (4.X/5), and [Vendor Name] appears in
our top-10 [category] rankings purely on editorial merit (we
don't sell rankings).

I wanted to reach out about our Verified Badge sponsorship -
$29/month gets you:

- Verified badge on your tool card across the directory
- Priority placement in /best/[category] (editorial still
  ranks winners, sponsored sits after)
- Dofollow backlink to [vendor-domain]
- Pricing updates we keep synced monthly

Full details: https://mytheai.com/advertise

Our traffic is early-stage (just migrated infrastructure this
month) but the audience is high-intent: readers hitting
/tools/[slug] are 2-3 clicks from a purchase decision. Our
compare pages especially drive high-intent /go/ clicks.

Would a quick 15-min call next week work? Or happy to send
over details if async preferred.

Best,
John
Founder, MytheAi
mytheai.com | john@mytheai.com

PS - if not now, when's a better moment? Q1 budget cycles?
Happy to loop back then.
```

---

## Personalization checklist (before sending)

- [ ] Replace `[Vendor Name]` with correct brand name (e.g., "Fireflies.ai" not "Fireflies")
- [ ] Replace `[First Name]` with contact's first name (find on LinkedIn)
- [ ] Replace `[slug]` with their actual URL slug (e.g., `fireflies-ai`)
- [ ] Replace `[category]` with their primary category (`meeting notes`, `writing AI`, `video AI`)
- [ ] Replace `4.X/5` with actual rating from catalog
- [ ] Replace `[vendor-domain]` with their website
- [ ] Verify link `/tools/[slug]` actually works before send

---

## When NOT to use this template

- Enterprise target (Fortune 500) - needs custom pitch
- Vendor already paying affiliate commissions >$500/mo - propose sponsored on top, not replacement
- Vendor just raised Series D+ - may dismiss $29 as too small; use template-02 or template-03

---

## Follow-up sequence (if no reply)

```
Hi [First Name],

Short bump on this - any thoughts? Happy to send more detail
or hop on 10 min quick call.

John
```

### Day 7 - closing loop
```
Hi [First Name],

Last ping - if the Verified Badge isn't right for [Vendor Name]
right now, I'm curious what sponsored formats you've seen work
in your category. Always learning.

John
```
