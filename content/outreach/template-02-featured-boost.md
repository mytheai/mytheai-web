# Email Template 02 - Featured Boost $99/week

**Use for**: Vendors in product launch mode, seasonal campaigns, higher-budget SaaS, direct competitors to someone we're already featuring.

**Target reply rate**: 20-25% (urgency angle helps).
**Target close rate**: 15% of replies.

---

## Subject line variations

- `Featured slot in /best/[category] - [competitor] just took one`
- `[Vendor Name] launch? Featured Boost timing on MytheAi`
- `Short pitch: Featured placement for [Vendor Name] in Q4`

The "competitor just took one" variant is highest-converting IF it's true (don't fake it - creates distrust if they verify).

---

## Email body

```
Hi [First Name],

John Pham at MytheAi. We're an AI tools directory with
editorial rankings (no pay-to-rank) - John hand-tested
[Vendor Name] and the full review is at
https://mytheai.com/tools/[slug].

Reaching out because our Featured Boost slots in the
/[category] category open up weekly ($99/week), and [Vendor
Name] is a strong fit:

- Top of /best/[category] money page (above fold on mobile)
- Homepage Featured section rotation
- Bold border + "Sponsored" tag (editorial integrity)
- Weekly click + impression report

Why Featured over Verified Badge:
- Short-term burst instead of monthly commitment
- Fits product launches, seasonal campaigns, funding news
- Can book 2-4 weeks for a focused push

I'd suggest 2 weeks ($198) to test. We can lock the slot for
[specific date range - mention next calendar quarter].

Our /advertise page has all tiers: https://mytheai.com/advertise

Traffic context: early-stage post-migration, but readers on
/best/[category] are deep in evaluation mode (10x higher
intent than general directory traffic).

Open to a 15-min call? Or async email thread works too.

Best,
John
Founder, MytheAi
mytheai.com | john@mytheai.com
```

---

## Variant for product-launch moment

Insert after paragraph 2:

```
Timing note: I saw [Vendor Name]'s recent [launch / funding /
product update] via [source]. Our Featured Boost would compound
that launch window with discovery-mode traffic from users
actively searching "best [category] AI tool."

Would love to time a 2-week slot around your announcement.
```

Use when you can specifically reference something they just
shipped (ProductHunt launch, blog post, Series X announcement).

---

## Personalization checklist

- [ ] Vendor name + slug + category correct
- [ ] First name of contact
- [ ] If using "competitor" subject, verify competitor actually in talks (or remove)
- [ ] If using launch variant, specific launch + source cited
- [ ] 2-week lock dates = next calendar month (gives them planning time)

---

## Common fit profiles

| Vendor pattern | Why Featured Boost fits |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Just raised Series A/B | Marketing budget available, need visibility ROI |
| Launching new product tier | Discovery moment matters |
| Seasonal (end of year, Q1 budget) | Short-burst spend fits budget cycles |
| Direct competitor to Verified vendor | FOMO/competitive pressure |

## Follow-up sequence (if no reply)

Day 3: `[First Name], 2 slots left in /best/[category] for Q4 - should I hold one for [Vendor Name]?`

Day 7: `Timing may not be right - circling back in Q1 2027. Would a January slot be better? Our budget planning season.`

Day 14: Archive. Can revisit on their next launch.
