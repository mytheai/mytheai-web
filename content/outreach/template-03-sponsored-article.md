# Email Template 03 - Sponsored Article $199

**Use for**: Content-heavy SaaS (SEO tools, writing tools), vendors with substantial content marketing teams, higher ticket deals.

**Target reply rate**: 10-15% (higher commitment ask).
**Target close rate**: 25% of replies (self-selected buyers).

---

## Subject line variations

- `Co-authored article on MytheAi - [Vendor Name] feature?`
- `[Vendor Name] + "How to pick an AI [category] tool" - editorial collab?`
- `Sponsored editorial on MytheAi - permanent SEO backlink`

Content-marketer language works best (co-authored, editorial, permanent).

---

## Email body

```
Hi [First Name],

John Pham, founder of MytheAi (593-tool AI directory,
editorial rankings only - no pay-to-rank).

Specific pitch for [Vendor Name]: we publish Sponsored
Articles at $199/piece, and I think there's a strong fit
for a co-authored editorial piece around:

"How to Pick an AI [Category] Tool in 2026"
OR
"[Vendor Name] vs [top-competitor] vs [top-competitor]: An
Honest Breakdown"

Format:
- 800-1200 words, our editorial style
- You're the hero example (clearly labeled "Sponsored")
- Permanent URL in /blog with SEO metadata
- Dofollow backlinks to [vendor-domain] and your pricing page
- 1 newsletter mention when it goes live
- Syndicated to Bing + Yandex via our IndexNow infrastructure

Our hands-on review of [Vendor Name] is already live
(https://mytheai.com/tools/[slug]) - the sponsored article
is a parallel, dedicated piece with you shaping the angle.

Price: $199 one-time (lower than typical SaaS blog
sponsorship because our audience is pre-converted - they
land on MytheAi actively comparing tools, not casual
scrollers).

Full tier breakdown: https://mytheai.com/advertise

What would make this a yes for you? Happy to adjust angle,
scope, timeline.

Best,
John
Founder, MytheAi
mytheai.com | john@mytheai.com

PS - if $199 is below your typical content partnership tier,
we can scope to 1500+ words with case study integration at
$499. Just ask.
```

---

## Variant for writing-AI / SEO vendors specifically

Insert after the "Format" bullet:

```
Context on content distribution: our /blog articles typically
get indexed by Google within 48-72h (fresh after migration).
We also submit each new article to Bing/Yandex via IndexNow
auto-trigger, which gives Sponsored Articles 2-3x faster
distribution than typical SaaS blog placements.
```

Shows them you understand their world (SEO sophistication).

---

## Personalization checklist

- [ ] Replace `[top-competitor]` and `[top-competitor]` with ACTUAL named competitors (otherwise article angle is generic)
- [ ] Research their blog style - do they prefer tutorial, listicle, or opinion? Match your pitch
- [ ] If they have a style guide publicly, reference it: "we can match your voice if preferred"
- [ ] If they have a sponsored content program on their own blog, mention: "I noticed [Vendor Name] does similar sponsored collabs on your blog - this would be the reverse"

---

## When NOT to use this template

- Vendor doesn't have active blog = not content-focused, won't value article format
- Vendor already asks for $5000+ for similar placements - our $199 insults them, downgrade to Featured Boost
- Vendor has strict "no sponsored content" policy (visible on About page) - respect it, skip

## Follow-up sequence

Day 3: `[First Name], curious if the editorial angle resonates - happy to send a sample outline before you commit.`

Day 7: `Would $499 scope (1500 words + case study) land better? Or want me to pitch a different format entirely?`

Day 14: Archive. Add to Q1 2027 content partnership revisit list.

## Delivery timeline promise

Once contract signed:
- Day 1-3: Brief + draft outline
- Day 4-7: Draft 1 for vendor review
- Day 8-10: Revisions (max 1 round)
- Day 11-12: Final + publish
- Day 13: Newsletter mention + IndexNow ping

Total ~14 days brief-to-live. Can rush to 7 days for +$100 rush fee.
