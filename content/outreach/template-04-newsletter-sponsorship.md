# Email Template 04 - Newsletter Sponsorship $299/send

**Use when**: Email list has 500+ engaged subscribers. **NOT READY YET** (list is building via NewsletterInlineForm just launched 2026-10-08). Expected ready: ~2027-02 at current rate.

**Target reply rate**: 25-35% (vendors know newsletter conversions).
**Target close rate**: 30% of replies.

Keeping this template drafted so when list qualifies, outreach is instant.

---

## Pre-qualification before using

Only pitch once:
- [ ] 500+ confirmed subscribers (via Supabase newsletter_subscribers count)
- [ ] 2+ newsletter editions sent with open rate >25%, click rate >3%
- [ ] At least 1 vendor case study (someone bought Verified Badge, mention them)

Pitching below these thresholds = burned bridges + "newsletter has no audience" reputation.

---

## Subject line variations

- `Newsletter sponsorship slot - [Vendor Name] + [relevant audience segment]`
- `Our Wednesday 3-Pick: want [Vendor Name] in next week's edition?`
- `[Vendor Name] + MytheAi newsletter: 1 slot/week`

---

## Email body

```
Hi [First Name],

John at MytheAi. Quick pitch:

Our weekly newsletter "The Wednesday 3-Pick" goes to [X]
engaged subscribers - founders, marketers, and operators
actively evaluating AI tools. Last 4 editions averaged
[Y]% open rate and [Z]% click-through.

I'm opening one sponsored slot per edition at $299/send:

- Dedicated 80-120 word section (we write it, you approve)
- Direct link to your tool or specific landing page
- Click tracking + opens report within 48h of send
- Position: between pick #1 and pick #2 (highest attention)

Why fit for [Vendor Name]:
- Our hand-tested review of [Vendor Name] is already live
  (https://mytheai.com/tools/[slug]) - subscribers may
  recognize the brand
- [Audience segment, e.g. "most subscribers evaluate 3-5 AI
  tools per month"] matches your ICP

Available slots: [specific weeks].

Full details: https://mytheai.com/advertise

Interested? Reply with preferred slot date and I'll send
over the brief template.

Best,
John
Founder, MytheAi
mytheai.com | john@mytheai.com
```

---

## Personalization fields to fill

- [X] = exact subscriber count (don't round up - vendors verify via click rates)
- [Y]% = actual open rate (industry benchmark: 25-35% for engaged lists)
- [Z]% = actual click-through rate (benchmark: 2-5%)
- Specific weeks = give 2-3 week options so they have calendar flexibility

---

## Pricing ladder (negotiation flexibility)

| List size | Starting price | Floor |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| 500-1500 subs | $299 | $199 |
| 1500-3000 subs | $499 | $349 |
| 3000-5000 subs | $699 | $499 |
| 5000+ subs | $999 | $699 |

Floor = minimum you accept to not devalue slot. If vendor pushes below floor, decline and keep slot open.

---

## Package upsells (close bigger)

**Standard $299**: 1 send, 1 slot
**Growth $599**: 2 sends over 4 weeks (same vendor, same slot)
**Repeat Partner $999/month**: 4 sends/month = 1 per week

Repeat Partner = recurring MRR, worth negotiating hard for.

---

## Follow-up sequence

Day 3: `[First Name], our next 2 slots fill fast - want me to hold [specific date] while you check with your team?`

Day 7: `Different angle - would you be interested in editing/co-writing the sponsored copy? Some vendors prefer that control.`

Day 14: `Last check - if the standard tier doesn't fit, we can custom-scope. Open to any format that works for [Vendor Name].`

Day 21: Archive. Follow up when they next launch something newsworthy.

---

## Red flags to watch

If vendor asks for:
- Exclusive category (e.g. "we're the only writing-AI featured in Q4") - NEVER agree, breaks editorial integrity
- Guaranteed conversion count - NEVER agree, newsletter sends != guaranteed sales
- Removing "Sponsored" label - immediate decline, breach of our ethics
- Free trial issue - we don't give free sends to prove value, period
