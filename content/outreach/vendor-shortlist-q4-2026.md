## Finding the right contact

For each vendor, find the correct partnerships/marketing email in this order:

1. **Website footer** - look for "Partners", "Press", "Media kit", "Affiliate Program"
2. **LinkedIn** - search `{company} marketing partnerships` - email their head of growth
3. **Standard emails to try**: `partners@`, `partnerships@`, `press@`, `marketing@`, `growth@`
4. **If no response in 7 days**: find CEO/CMO on LinkedIn + send connection request with 1-line soft pitch

Avoid `info@` or `support@` - routes to customer success, not marketing.
